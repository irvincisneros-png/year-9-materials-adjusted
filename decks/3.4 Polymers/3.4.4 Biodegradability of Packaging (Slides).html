Packaging Piles Up

Billions of bags, bottles and wrappers every year

Most packaging ends up in landfill or the ocean. Knowing which materials break down — and which don't — helps us make smarter choices.

BIODEGRADABILITY

What Is Biodegradability?

Ability of a material to be broken down by living things
Microorganisms (bacteria and fungi) do the breaking
Products depend on conditions: aerobic breakdown can form CO₂, water and biomass; anaerobic processes can also form methane
DECOMPOSERS

Microorganisms: The Clean-Up Crew

Bacteria and fungi live in soil and water
They eat organic materials and break them apart
Most conventional plastics resist microbial attack, although their surfaces can still host microbes

Fast vs Slow: Breakdown Times

Fast
Leaf — days to weeks
Paper — weeks to months
Cardboard — weeks to months
Slow
Plastic bag (LDPE) — can persist for a long time; estimates depend on conditions
PLA bioplastic — may need specified industrial composting conditions
Most plastics — often resist biodegradation and may fragment instead

BIOPLASTICS

What About Bioplastics (PLA)?

PLA is commonly made from fermented plant-derived sugars
Many PLA products need a specified industrial composting system
Outside its specified composting system, its breakdown may be much slower
PRACTICAL METHOD

The Bury-and-Dig-Up Test

Bury samples in moist soil for a few weeks
Dig them up and compare to how they started
Score: broken down a lot / a little / not at all
EXPERIMENTAL DESIGN

Why Control the Conditions?

Same soil — same microorganisms for each sample
Same moisture — so only the material changes
Fair test: one variable, everything else equal
PRACTICE

Packaging Claims Practice

For each claim, ask what it means and what evidence or conditions are required.

BiodegradableMicrobes can break it down; ask which conditions and end products are supported by evidence.
CompostableIt must break down in the specified home or industrial composting system.
DegradableIt may only fragment; this does not show complete or safe breakdown.
RecyclableIt is useful only where local collection and processing facilities accept it.
