MATERIAL SCIENCE

Properties Help Us Choose

A property is a feature we can observe or test.
Examples: flexibility, stiffness, transparency and water resistance.
Suitability also depends on thickness, additives and shape.
KEY PROPERTIES

Key Properties Can Vary

Flexible — bends easily (e.g. shopping bag)
Stiff and strong — holds its shape (e.g. milk bottle)
See-through — light passes through (e.g. drink bottle)
SHARED PROPERTY

Plastics and Liquid Water

Almost ALL plastics are waterproof
Permeability varies with polymer, thickness and additives
Packaging must match the barrier needed

Thermoplastics and Thermosets

SOFTENS
Long chains with few cross-links
Softens when heated; can often be reshaped
Examples: LDPE, HDPE and PET
THERMOSET
Chains form a cross-linked network
Does not remelt; strong heating causes degradation
Examples: epoxy and many pan handles
COMMON POLYMERS

Common Thermoplastics

LDPE — flexible film and bags.
HDPE — stiff bottles and crates.
PET — clear bottles and food trays.

A polymer name does not guarantee one fixed set of properties.

RECYCLING CONTEXT

Recycling Is a System

Thermoplastics can be remelted and reshaped.
A resin code identifies type, not local acceptance.
Sorting and contamination affect recycling.

Check your local program before placing an item in the bin.
